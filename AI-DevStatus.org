* Key Design Decisions

- *The commands are one set* (2026-10-19), edited here only; every base on the host gets them by
  assembly. Rationale: the engine =AI-DevStatus.org= (Key Design Decisions) and its Stage 18.
- *Two layers*: BISOS-AI-Workflow (this repo), adopted by forks as-is; BISOS-Digital-Ecosystem-Skills
  (=aiTemplates-DE=), replaced by a fork's own.
- *=mother/= stays the install source only*; material about the templates (figures) lives in the
  engine, not here (=_nonTemplate_/= retired, engine 18.12).
- *The input repos are aiActivity projects themselves* (2026-10-20), with the circular links that
  implies: the templates are developed with the workflow they define.

- *Commit messages: the "69/90" format (2026-10-27, user decision)* --- in
  =mother/_claude/commands/bx-git-commit-msg.md= and the commands table of =mother/AI-WORKFLOW.org=:
  subject at most 69 characters (aim 60), body wrapped at 90, sized for magit in a split window;
  past tense; plain text; trailers =TODOs:= (WorkPlan anchors, =repo:anchor= for another repo's),
  =Requires:= (cross-repo dependency), =Co-Authored-By:=. The command finds the TODOs from the staged
  diff against the WorkPlan and its archives. Rationale: TODO anchors are stable even when archived,
  so a commit points at its AI Results instead of repeating them. Blee side (=blee/env3=,
  =plus/bcpg:magitPlus.el=, committed): the same limits in =git-commit-mode=, a confirmation on an
  over-long subject, a narrower log margin, and =c c= offers =.git/CLAUDE_COMMIT_MSG= (renamed
  =.used= after the commit). An existing command was edited: no re-assembly needed.
